Kubescape's Journey to Incubation: Celebrating our Community and a Secure Kubernetes Future We are thrilled to share that Kubescape has officially been accepted as a CNCF Incubating project. This milestone is a significant achievement for the project. Kubescape began in 2021 as a fun project to scan for compliance with NSA-CISA Kubernetes hardening guidelines.
